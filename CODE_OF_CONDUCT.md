# Community conduct

Treat participants respectfully. Discuss code and ideas without personal attacks,
harassment, discrimination, threats or disclosure of private information. Keep
issues and pull requests relevant and avoid spam.

The maintainer may edit or remove inappropriate content and limit participation.
For ordinary concerns, contact the maintainer through the repository's available
GitHub contact facilities. Do not post private details publicly; a dedicated private
conduct contact must be established before opening community participation.
No response time or continuous moderation coverage is promised.
