boards, local engine play and analysis, clocks, PGN/FEN tools, bookmarks and

## Maintainer steps after separate publication approval

1. Resolve all release blockers; prepare the exact source and binary manifest.
2. Create the itch.io page only after approval. Replace every placeholder above.
3. Configure **AUD 10**. itch.io's Direct Payments supports AUD; its collected
   payout mode handles transactions in USD. Confirm the actual checkout currency
   and tax presentation rather than silently substituting USD 10. See
   [itch.io payment documentation](https://itch.io/docs/creators/payments).
4. Keep paid binary access and free source access clearly distinguished. Place
   prominent working links to the exact application and dependency source archives
   on the binary page. Check the links signed out and retain their availability.
5. Upload only the approved installer and matching notices/manifest. Use itch.io
   as the sole official paid distribution channel. Do not upload paid installers
   to GitHub; source archives there remain free.
6. Do not attach a custom agreement restricting GPL redistribution. Avoid optional
   donation/sponsorship facilities and any promised update schedule. Confirm whether
   platform-level optional overpayment can be disabled without changing the price
   or channel strategy; do not promote tips or donations in the listing.
7. Verify a purchase/download/install workflow, source access, offline launch and
   notices on a clean machine before publicly announcing availability.

The checkout account, tax/payment setup and store controls require the maintainer;
no payment integration or external service changes were made by this preparation.
