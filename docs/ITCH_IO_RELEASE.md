# Withdrawn itch.io distribution plan

The maintainer decided on 2026-09-30 that OTBMaster3D will not use itch.io.
The former listing draft, AUD $10 price and platform-specific publication
checklist are withdrawn. No replacement distribution channel has been selected.
Review of itch.io terms is no longer a release blocker.

The remaining source-publication and licensing requirements are recorded in
[SOURCE_ACCESS.md](../SOURCE_ACCESS.md) and
[licensing closure](licensing/LICENSING-CLOSURE.md).
